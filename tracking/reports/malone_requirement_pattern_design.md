# Requirement pattern design

## When selected

Strong “required / mandatory / obligation” question cues and/or requirement-like normalized units.

## Answer shape

1. Disclaimer + pattern label  
2. **Bottom line** (best plain-language summary, else first excerpt sentence)  
3. **Who must act** (distinct `applies_to_role` values)  
4. **Requirement strength** (`requirement_level` values)  
5. **Conditions**, **exceptions**, **reporting/escalation** strings when present  
6. **Sources** section: full legal citations or policy segments with excerpts and normalized blocks (legacy fidelity)

Legal retains full excerpt under each citation in the Sources section.
