# Department Intake — Safety & Governance

1. **Intake answers are not automatic truth** — They are draft organizational memory until reviewed/promoted under company-knowledge governance.
2. **Operations map rows are reviewable** — Metadata flags `from_intake`; stewards can cross-check against ingested SOPs/policies.
3. **Source-grounded docs outrank chat** — `evidence_precedence_rank` places legal and ingested segments above `intake_session_memory`.
4. **Deterministic legal behavior** — Intake APIs do not alter `malone_service` legal delivery paths.
5. **Follow-ups improve completeness, not authority** — Questions surface gaps; they do not certify correctness.
