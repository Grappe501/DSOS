# Review loop safety and precedence

1. **Evidence first** — citations, chunks, and deterministic legal formatters behave as before; governance never replaces excerpt text.
2. **Human feedback is secondary** — it adjusts trust labels, review columns, and advisory promotion hints.
3. **Auditability** — append-only events; rejected artifacts remain queryable.
4. **Scenario priors** — approved scenarios receive a **bounded** similarity boost only when version-conflict suppression does not apply.
5. **Rejected normalized units** — remain blocked from normalized augmentation (`unit_is_blocked`).
